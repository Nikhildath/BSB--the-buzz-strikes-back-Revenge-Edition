Buzz Strikes Back

A fully automated mosquito trap that makes mosquitoes experience their own annoying buzzing sound, then zaps them. Built with 2x ESP32, ESP-NOW, I2S audio, OLED emotic faces, and a web dashboard. Because science has gone too far.

View on GitHub How It Works
The Problem
That doesn't exist
😤

Mosquitoes are annoying

They fly around all day making that high-pitched buzzing sound right next to your ear at 3 AM. But they never have to listen to it themselves. This is deeply unfair.

💡

The Solution

We built a trap that records their own wingbeat frequency (400-600Hz) and blasts it back at them through a speaker. Lure them in with blue LED, detect them, make them suffer, then zap them. Justice served.

How It Works
5 steps to mosquito revenge
1

Lure

Blue LED light at 470nm wavelength attracts mosquitoes to the trap

2

Detect

IR break-beam sensor detects when a mosquito enters the trap zone

3

Torture

MAX98357A amplifier plays their own buzzing sound back at them through a speaker

4

Zap

High-voltage grid fires via MOSFET driver, eliminating the mosquito
